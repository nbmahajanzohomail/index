MODEL STATUS

Explainable index pressure, with confidence kept in check.

The frozen v1 rule baseline gives an index-level next-session label: rise, fall, range, no edge, or insufficient data. It shows price, futures, options, and cross-index evidence separately. It does not select a strike or prescribe an option position.

Confidence stays low until sample history and evidence breadth pass the fixed gates; even “moderate” is explicitly uncalibrated. The historical check reports chronological hit rates and coverage, and does not estimate profit after option costs. No stock symbols, constituent data, or indices outside the seven requested are included.

ON YOUR SCHEDULE

Download reports from NSE and BSE

One From/Through range is used for both exchanges. Each request fetches fresh reports for the selected dates. Stored rows are replaced only after each report passes completeness checks. A failed or partial response leaves existing rows intact and can be retried. Nothing downloads in the background.

Reports to download

Up to 93 calendar days per request. Selected NSE and BSE reports are refreshed for the same range, including dates already in the database. F&O reports are fetched by trading date, checked in memory, and filtered to the five NSE plus two BSE indices; raw all-market files are not saved.

Coverage: NSE reports supply NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, and NIFTYNXT50. BSE supplies Sensex and Bankex. Both exchanges' combined derivatives bhavcopies are processed in memory, and only the seven selected index contracts are stored.
